Our company

We make privacy provable.

NigraniAI (nigrani, "watchful oversight") builds the compliance layer India's privacy law needs: software that watches what systems really do with personal data, explains it, and proves it.

Our mission

Help every Indian business meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act from evidence, not paperwork: continuously, in plain language, and without ever putting customer data at risk.

Our vision

A country where any company can prove, on any day, that it respects the personal data of the people it serves, and where people can trust that it does.

Our story

From a question to a working system.

The question

"Can a company actually see what its systems do with personal data?"

With the DPDP Act passed in 2023, we asked a simple question. The honest answer for most businesses was no: compliance lived in spreadsheets and yearly audits.

The idea

Read the systems, not the paperwork

Instead of asking teams what they do, NigraniAI reads the live logs and database, read-only, and checks every rule of the Act against real activity.

The pilot

A real e-commerce company

We piloted NigraniAI on a live e-commerce stack with microservices. On day one it found a service reading payment and customer data with no declared purpose.

Today

Live, in India

The full platform runs on secure cloud servers in Mumbai: eleven tools, explainable machine learning and tamper-evident evidence.

Next

Built to scale

A streaming engine for very high log volumes, connectors for more databases and clouds, and coverage for children's data and Significant Data Fiduciary duties.

What we stand for

Principles we build by.

Read-only, always

We never write to a client's systems or copy customer records out. Trust starts with not touching what isn't ours.

Evidence over claims

Every finding comes from real activity and cites its section of the law. If we can't show it, we don't say it.

Explainable AI

Our models say what they saw and why they flagged it. No black boxes in a compliance tool.

Data minimised

Personal values are masked before anyone sees them; our AI works on summaries, never on customer records.

Built for India

Designed around the DPDP Act and Rules, Indian identifiers, and hosting in India.

People decide

NigraniAI drafts and recommends; your team reviews and acts. Accountability stays with people.
