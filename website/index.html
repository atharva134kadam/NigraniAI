Understand the DPDP Act

India's privacy law, explained simply.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 set how businesses may collect, use, protect and erase the personal data of people in India.

Data Principal

The person the data is about: your customer. They have rights to information, correction, erasure and grievance redress.

Data Fiduciary

The business that decides why and how data is processed. It carries the duties, and the penalties.

Data Processor

Anyone processing data on a Fiduciary's behalf, such as a cloud or payment provider. The Fiduciary stays responsible.

Data Protection Board

The authority that receives breach reports, conducts inquiries and imposes penalties.

Does the DPDP Act apply to my business?

If you process digital personal data of people in India, it almost certainly does: e-commerce, fintech, SaaS, healthcare, education and more. It also applies to processing outside India when you offer goods or services to people in India.

What counts as personal data?

Any data about an individual who can be identified by or in relation to it: names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, identifiers such as Aadhaar or PAN, and data linked to them such as orders or payments.

Do we need to change our website or apps to use NigraniAI?

No. NigraniAI connects read-only to the logs and database you already have. It never writes to your systems and needs no code inside your apps.

Does customer data leave our systems?

No customer records are copied out. Personal values are masked before they reach the dashboard, the PII language model runs on the NigraniAI server, and the AI assistant only sees minimised summaries.

Is NigraniAI legal advice?

No. It is a monitoring and evidence system that maps your activity to the Act and the Rules and cites each section. Use it alongside your legal and compliance team.
